Privacy and account control

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

IVACO32934 Drive Backup is a single-operator application for storing encrypted copies of a designated ERPNext deployment in the operator’s own Google Drive. It is not a public-facing multi-user service.

1. Data the utility handles

Backup archives may contain business database records, configuration and attachments from the operator’s ERPNext installation. Archives are encrypted with age before upload. In operating the backup, the utility processes file names, sizes, timestamps and Google Drive file identifiers needed for upload and integrity checks.

2. Google permissions and purpose

The utility requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission to create, list, download and manage its own app-created backup objects in the authorized Google Drive account. It does not need unrestricted access to unrelated Google Drive files, Gmail, Contacts or other Google user data. Drive access is used only for backup, integrity verification and operator-directed recovery.

3. Where data and credentials are stored

Encrypted archive objects are stored in a private folder in the operator’s Google Drive account. OAuth credentials and the refresh token are stored in access-restricted configuration on an operator-controlled backup server. The private age decryption identity is held separately on an operator-controlled device, with an offline encrypted recovery copy; it is not uploaded to Google Drive or published here.

4. Sharing and disclosure

The utility does not sell user data, publish enterprise backup files, share backup content with unrelated third parties, or use Google Drive data for advertising or profiling. Encrypted objects are transmitted to and stored by Google Drive at the operator’s direction.

5. Retention and deletion

Encrypted objects remain in the operator-controlled Google Drive until the operator deletes them or explicitly configures a separate retention policy. Revoking application access does not automatically remove existing Drive objects. The operator can delete archives from Drive and revoke authorization through Google Account security settings.

6. Security limitations

Encryption protects stored backup content against ordinary unauthorized reading, but no system can guarantee perfect security. The operator is responsible for protecting access to Google, the server, the private decryption identity and offline recovery passphrase.

7. Contact

For privacy or authorization questions, use the developer support email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
