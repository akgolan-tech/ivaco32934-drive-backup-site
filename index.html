Private, operator-controlled backup

Encrypted ERPNext backups in the operator’s Google Drive

IVACO32934-Drive-Backup is a small, single-operator utility that stores encrypted backups of an ERPNext deployment in a private, owner-managed Google Drive folder.

How your data is handled

Designed for limited access

Encryption before upload

Backup archives are encrypted with age before they are uploaded. The decryption identity is retained separately by the operator and is not stored in Google Drive.

Limited Drive permission

The utility requests drive.file to create, read and manage only its own app-created Drive files and folders. It does not request blanket access to other Drive content.

No public backup sharing

The site is an informational page only. It provides no login form, accepts no uploaded files and never exposes backup archives or enterprise records.

About this OAuth app

This page explains why the operator authorizes Google Drive access. The utility uses the Google Drive API solely for encrypted offsite backup operations in the operator’s own account. Files are kept private and under the account owner’s control.

For data handling, retention, revocation and contact details, read the Privacy Policy. The designated developer support email is displayed by Google on the OAuth consent screen.
