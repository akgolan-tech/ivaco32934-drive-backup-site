Purpose

IVACO32934 Drive Backup exists solely to manage encrypted backup archives for an ERPNext deployment in the authorized operator’s private Google Drive. This website documents the app and does not offer a hosted service or accounts for the public.

Authorized use

Only the designated operator may authorize and operate the backup utility. The utility requests limited Drive access for its own app-created files. Access must not be used to retrieve unrelated Google account content.

Operator responsibilities

The operator controls the Google account, backup schedule, archive retention, encryption key recovery and integrity checks. The operator should test restoration regularly and protect recovery materials independently of Drive.

Availability

Backup operations depend on the availability of the ERPNext installation, the controlled backup server and Google Drive. Availability or future recovery success cannot be guaranteed by this informational website.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for details on Google data access, encryption, storage and removal.
